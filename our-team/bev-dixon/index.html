Bev's Story

Having co-founded, been an Operations Director and COO of leading operators in the UK, Bev‘s knowledge of park operations is second to none. With 24 years experience in the sector, Bev has led large teams of General Managers, managed HR, health and safety, retail and much more. She completely understands everything required to manage operations and people.

With extensive experience in planning applications, developing brilliant local community relationships, commercial contracts and legal matters, you know you’re in safe hands with Bev. Her tenacious nature ensures that plans get actioned and her ability to project manage multiple projects is truly remarkable.

A fun, hands on leader that never takes herself too seriously, Bev’s never scared to muck in and she develops long lasting relationships as she truly cares and loves seeing the people around her thrive. One of the most respected female operators in the sector, Bev has paved the way for many.
