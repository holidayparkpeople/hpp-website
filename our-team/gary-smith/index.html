Gary's Story

This guy is happiest with a camera in his hand! Gary is passionate about the right photos, videos and creative content for your brand, he really does care. He’ll spend all the time needed getting to understand brands, what they represent and how they make customers feel. Then like magic, he’ll bring it all to life with design, photos and videos. You’ll be forgiven for feeling like he’s in your head when you spend time with him.

It’s no secret that most people feel awkward or uncomfortable in front of a camera, but not when Gary is around. He has a natural way of making people feel at ease and has some unorthodox methods for getting that perfect shot. One of the coolest guys we know, he has the patience of a saint with that creative edge that you just can’t put your finger on.
