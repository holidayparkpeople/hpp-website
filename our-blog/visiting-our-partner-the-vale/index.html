Our Visit to The Vale: A Sneak Peak at Ohana Mua’s New Destination in Cornwall

Hey,

Last week, The Holiday Park People hit the road (and I mean really hit the road - 14 hours of it) to visit something very special: The Vale, Ohana Mua’s brand-new destination opening this July in Looe, Cornwall. Safe to say it was worth every minute

There’s nothing quite like seeing The Vale for the first time. Experiencing it through our own eyes - or 'tourist eyes', as we like to say at HPP - gave us a real feel for the destination we’ve been bringing to life through marketing these past few months.

Myself (Emily, hi!), Matt, Stacey, Ellie and, last but not least, Paddy the cocker spaniel, were lucky enough to meet some of the fantastic team at Ohana while we were there. A special thank you to Karl, Simon, Pete, Matthew and Gemma for making us feel so welcome at The Vale

Now we want to bring you along for the ride - to share what we saw, what we felt, and why we’re so excited about what’s coming. Keep reading to get excited with us!

Day One:

We set off bright and early on Tuesday morning with all the essentials: camera kit, hiking gear, dog treats, and Matt and Stacey’s must-have: the cheese and marmite toasted sandwich, a road trip classic. Before we knew it, we arrived in Polperro, just a stone’s throw from The Vale.

Polperro was like stepping into a postcard: winding streets, fairytale cottages, and a charming harbour. We stayed in the heart of the village which was the perfect base for an evening of exploring, soaking in the salty sea air, and capturing some beautiful shots of the local area.

Day Two:

On Wednesday morning, we set off to explore Looe, the idyllic coastal town that’s home to The Vale. First things first: Bowden Bakery. Paddy, who was gearing up for a day in front of the camera, was treated to a doggy cruffin. And honestly? He earned it.

Once fuelled up, we all headed straight to The Vale for our first proper look around. It was the moment we’d all been waiting for - the chance to walk the grounds, explore the lodges and cottages, and see the destination we’ve been marketing come to life. The Ohana team gave us a warm welcome and behind-the-scenes tour, showing us all the thought, care and precision that goes into delivering an exceptional guest experience.

As someone who’s new to the holiday park world, stepping into the lodges at The Vale blew my mind, and my expectations, out of the water. We visited the Cardinham Lodge and the Lerryn Lodge, and both completely redefined what I thought holiday homes in the UK were like. From the moment I walked in, I was struck by the attention to detail, the high-end interiors, and the sheer quality throughout. Outside, things got even more impressive: private hot tubs, outdoor showers, and those iconic copper baths. And the best part? Ohana have managed to deliver all of this while staying true to their ethos: The Vale is an energy-positive destination that blends sustainability with style in a way that feels meaningful and effortless.

With cameras and gimbals in hand, we captured interiors, exteriors, scenic views and footage of the surrounding landscape. Standing there among the greenery and wildflowers, it was clear that The Vale isn’t just another holiday park, it’s a destination designed to help people switch off, reconnect, and enjoy a slower pace of life.

After soaking it all in and snapping as much content as we could, we split into two teams to cover more ground. Matt and Stacey stayed on site at The Vale with the Ohana crew to focus on more detailed photography and video of the accommodation. Meanwhile, Ellie and I made our way into Looe to explore the coastal town guests will soon be discovering for themselves.

We wandered through quirky little shops and stopped at colourful window displays, chatted with friendly locals about their favourite places in Looe - The Sardine Factory and Catch were the top picks for seafood and fish and chips - and tried (unsuccessfully) to dodge the seagulls who really wanted to try our Cornish ice creams. We also did a bit of souvenir shopping and supported some lovely local businesses before rejoining Matt and Stacey to go through everything we’d captured that day. The creative juices were well and truly flowing. We were all buzzing with ideas, excited about The Vale and how we could bring it to life in our marketing ahead of the grand opening.

Day Three:

We hit the road Thursday morning and the hours just flew by, but it gave me enough time to reflect on our visit to The Vale. I know I can speak for everyone, even Paddy, when I say seeing The Vale in person completely changed the way we think and talk about it. It’s not just a location on a website or a name in a marketing plan - it’s a living, breathing destination with so much to offer.

From the peaceful charm of Polperro to the bustling magic of Looe, this trip gave us a fresh new perspective. We’ve come away with cameras full of content, heads full of ideas, and a real appreciation for what Ohana has created.

We can’t wait to come back when The Vale is open and alive with guests. Whether it’s for a quiet getaway in nature, a family escape, or a base to explore the Cornish coastline, we know people are going to fall in love with it just like we did.
