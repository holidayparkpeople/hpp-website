The Holiday Park People x ParcVu Partnership

Hey,

There’s a lot happening in the holiday park world right now. Systems are evolving, guest expectations are rising, and operators are under more pressure than ever to get their tech, operations and customer experience working properly together.

And if we’re honest, that transition isn’t always smooth..

That’s why we’re so pleased to be able to talk about our new partnership with ParcVu powered by Booking Experts.

We officially joined forces as consultation partners. Not just to “work together”, but to actually help holiday parks make better decisions, implement better systems, and feel properly supported while doing it.

Because good technology on its own isn’t enough. It has to work in the real world of holiday parks. And that means with real teams, real pressures, and real day-to-day operations.

That’s where this partnership comes in.

Why we partnered with ParcVu powered by Booking Experts

We’ve worked in and around the holiday park sector long enough to see a consistent challenge.

A park invests in a new system…

It’s powerful, it looks great on paper…

But the rollout doesn’t quite land.

Training gets rushed. Teams fall back into old habits. Processes don’t fully align. And suddenly, the “solution” doesn’t feel like a solution at all.

That’s exactly the gap we wanted to help close.

ParcVu powered by Booking Experts brings the technology: a platform designed specifically for holiday parks, where you can manage sales, bookings and park operations all in one place.

We bring the sector experience: the operational understanding, the commercial reality, and the hands-on knowledge of how parks actually run day to day.

Together, that combination matters.

Because it means parks aren’t just buying software. They’re getting support to actually make it work properly within their business.

So what does our partnership mean for holiday parks?

Simply put, we’ll help parks transition to better systems, so they won’t have to figure it out alone.

We work alongside parks as consultation partners, helping teams with:

  • Additional strategic or operational guidance
  • Optimising performance
  • Making the most of the platform in their day-to-day operations
  • Getting confident and comfortable using the platform

It’s not about overcomplicating things. It’s about making things work properly.

And sometimes that just means having the right people in the room who understand both the tech and the sector.

Built for parks, backed by people who understand them

One of the things we really rate about ParcVu powered by Booking Experts is that it’s built specifically for holiday parks.

That sounds simple, but it makes a huge difference.

Holiday parks aren’t standard hospitality businesses. They’ve got ownership models, seasonal demand shifts, complex guest journeys, and operational layers that don’t exist anywhere else in quite the same way.

The platform reflects that, but even the best-built system still needs the right implementation behind it. That’s why our partnership makes a huge difference.

A conversation we really enjoyed having

We also had the chance to sit down with Andrew Cressey and James Bouldin for a podcast episode that honestly turned into one of our favourites.

We spoke about technology in the holiday park industry, what operators are really struggling with, and how systems need to evolve to genuinely support parks - not slow them down.

We also talked openly about the partnership itself and why consultation matters just as much as the tech.

If you work in the sector, it’s well worth a listen.

Watch the podcast on YouTube

You can also find it on all major streaming platforms.

Why this matters right now

There’s a shift happening across the industry.

More parks are reviewing their systems. More are questioning whether what they’ve got is really working. And more are looking for solutions that don’t just “add software”, but actually improve how the business runs.

But the reality is, change is hard without support.

That’s what this partnership is about - making that change easier, more structured, and more successful.

Not just delivering a system and walking away.

Staying involved. Making sure it works. Helping teams feel confident using it. And ensuring it actually delivers long-term value.

Final thought

At the heart of this partnership, it still comes back to something simple: better systems only work when people feel supported using them.

That’s what we’re building with ParcVu powered by Booking Experts.

Technology built for parks.

Backed by people who understand parks.

Focused on helping parks actually succeed with it.

Til next time 💙
