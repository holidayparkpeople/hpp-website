Exceptional holidays with heart are now available to book in Cornwall

Hey,

“Ohana” - a Hawaiian word that means “family". It can also refer to the people who support and sustain you, even if you aren't related to them.

We love and wholeheartedly resonate with the values of Ohana Mua, a strategic partner that we’ve been working alongside throughout the last 6 months.

Like a supportive family member would be, we are bursting with pride to see the project the Ohana Mua team has been working on tirelessly for the last 6 months, launch for holiday bookings. We mean it when we say their approach to standards, service and green initiatives is truly inspiring.

We cannot wait to stay at The Vale at Peakswater and experience it first hand. We know we’d better be quick as there’s no doubt this location and accommodation is going to see overwhelming demand. The best location in Cornwall in 2025? We’d say so!

The Vale at Peakswater is a beautiful riverside location just a stones throw from Looe in Cornwall. A peaceful countryside retreat surrounded by amazing scenery with an idyllic harbour town on your doorstep - what’s not to love?

We’ve been lucky enough to visit the park a couple of times and have witnessed first hand the investment and attention to detail taking place. Accommodation that’s been refurbished to the highest of standards, innovative technology that enhances but doesn’t hinder, energy positive accommodation, EV charging at every lodge and cottage, a big yes from us! And don’t get Stacey started on the outdoor copper bath tubs.

If you’re looking to experience something special in a beautiful part of the country in 2025 then we suggest you act fast, you can book on the Ohana Mua website from today

There’s no doubt this location is going to be top of the wishlist for many and we can’t wait to see what this exceptional bunch of people has in store next

To join the Ohana community you can sign up to their newsletter here and if you’re feeling social you can follow them on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. We’d recommend it, their journey is going to be a special one.

Til next time

Stacey & Matt
